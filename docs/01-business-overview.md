# 01 · Business Overview

## One-liner
**We get businesses recommended by AI assistants** such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Copilot. We measure what these assistants say about the brand, fix what keeps it out of their answers, and track progress every month.

## The problem
- Buyers increasingly ask AI assistants for recommendations ("best accounting software for a 10-person firm", "good orthodontist near me") instead of scrolling search results.
- An AI answer typically names **only a few businesses**. You are either in the answer or invisible.
- Ranking well on Google doesn't guarantee being in AI answers. AI assistants also weigh structured data, clear quotable content, third-party mentions, reviews, and whether their crawlers are allowed on the site.
- There is **no "AI Search Console"**. Businesses can't see what AI says about them.

## What we sell
A **done-for-you service**, powered by our own platform. Clients buy outcomes and reports. They don't need a software login.

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| AI visibility measurement | Ask each AI assistant the real questions customers ask, then record brand mentions, rank, citations, sentiment and competitor share of voice |
| AI-readiness audit | Technical and content audit of how AI crawlers see the website (17 checks, scored 0–100) |
| Optimisation | Implement the fixes: robots.txt, schema markup, llms.txt, FAQ content, rewrites, new content, third-party citations |
| Monitoring & reporting | Weekly tracking, plus a monthly white-label report with trends and next steps |

## Service model: one-time + recurring
1. **Free AI visibility check** (lead magnet, automated): an instant AI-readiness score on the website.
2. **AI Search Audit** (one-time, paid): baseline, competitor comparison, prioritised plan, strategy call.
3. **Monthly plan** (recurring retainer): implementation, content, weekly tracking, monthly report.

**Why recurring is justified:** AI answers change as models update and search the web live, competitors optimise too, and authority-building takes months. The client needs ongoing measurement to know where they stand.

## Positioning
- **Category name:** AI Search Optimisation (also called GEO, generative engine optimisation, or AEO, answer engine optimisation). Use "AI search optimisation" with clients, because it's the most understandable.
- **Against traditional SEO agencies:** we measure AI answers directly with our own tracking platform, not just Google rankings.
- **Against self-serve AI visibility SaaS tools:** we do the work and explain it in plain language. The client doesn't have to interpret dashboards.
- **Honesty as a differentiator:** we never guarantee AI recommendations. We guarantee transparency and effort.

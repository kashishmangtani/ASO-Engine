# 02 · Brand & Naming

**Status: decision pending.** Pick a name, run the checklist at the bottom, then record the result in the decisions log.

## What the name needs to do
1. Signal **AI + being recommended, cited or found**, without being tied to one AI brand (never put "ChatGPT" or "GPT" in the name, since that's a trademark risk).
2. Work for **both** a freelance service and a future SaaS product.
3. Be short, easy to spell after hearing it once, and available as a domain (.com, .ai or .io) and a LinkedIn page.
4. Sound credible to business owners and marketing heads, not gimmicky.

## Shortlist

| Name | Idea behind it | Pros | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Citeable** | Being cite-able by AI | Says exactly what we do, and reads well as a verb ("make your brand citeable") | Spelling variants (Citable or Citeable): buy both domains |
| **AnswerRank** | Ranking inside AI answers | Instantly understood by marketers who know SEO | Generic; check trademarks |
| **Mentionwise** | Smart about AI mentions | Friendly, broad | Longer; similar names may exist |
| **Recommendable** | Becoming the business AI recommends | Benefit-led, memorable | Long domain |
| **AskedFor** | When people ask AI, you're who they're asked for | Distinctive, story-friendly | Less self-explanatory |
| **Visibli** | Visibility in AI | Short, brandable | Invented spelling |
| **PromptPosition** | Your position in the answer to a prompt | Descriptive for marketers | Jargon to small-business owners |
| **NamedFirst** | Be the first name AI gives | Strong outcome promise | Can sound like a guarantee; use carefully |

## Recommendation
**Citeable**, with **AnswerRank** as the backup.
- "Citeable" describes the outcome (being cited and recommended by AI), works as a service brand now and a SaaS later, and gives an easy tagline:
  - *"Citeable: get recommended by AI."*
  - *"Make your business citeable."*
- If the domain or trademark is taken, go to the backup rather than settling for an awkward variant.

## Name launch checklist
- [ ] Domain available (.com preferred; .ai or .io acceptable). Buy the common misspelling too.
- [ ] Trademark search in your country and target markets (e.g. USPTO TESS, EUIPO, UK IPO, or your national registry)
- [ ] LinkedIn company page URL available
- [ ] Instagram, X and YouTube handles (nice to have)
- [ ] Search the name plus "AI" to make sure no direct competitor uses it
- [ ] Say it out loud to 5 people, then ask them to spell it
- [ ] Update `AGENCY_NAME`, `AGENCY_COLOR`, `AGENCY_WEBSITE` and `AGENCY_EMAIL` in `.env`

# 08 · Sales Playbook

## Sales process
```
Lead (free check / LinkedIn / referral)
  → Personal follow-up within 24h
  → Discovery call (20–30 min) with a LIVE AI test
  → Proposal (AI Search Audit, or straight to a retainer)
  → Audit delivered + strategy call
  → Retainer proposal
  → Onboarding (doc 09)
```

## Before the call (10 minutes of prep)
1. Create the prospect as a client in `/app` (or convert the lead).
2. Add 5–8 questions their customers would ask, plus 3 competitors.
3. Run a visibility check and an audit of their homepage.
4. Note 3 insights: who AI recommends instead, one technical blocker, one quick win.

## Discovery call script (20–30 min)
1. **Rapport & agenda (2 min):** "I'll ask a few questions, show you what AI says about you live, and if it makes sense we'll talk next steps."
2. **Their situation (5 min)**
   - How do new customers find you today?
   - What's a new customer worth to you?
   - Who are your main competitors?
   - Are you working with an SEO or marketing agency?
   - Have any customers mentioned finding you through ChatGPT or another AI?
3. **Live demo (8 min):** share your screen, ask an AI assistant their buyers' question, and show who it names. Then show the run results and audit score from the platform. *This is the moment that sells.*
4. **Implications (3 min):** "If [competitor] is the default answer when customers ask, what does that mean over the next year?"
5. **Solution (5 min):** Measure, then Fix, then Grow. Explain the audit and the monthly plan, and show a sample report.
6. **Next step (2 min):** propose the audit and book the strategy call date before hanging up.

## Objection handling
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "My customers don't use ChatGPT." | "Maybe not yet, but let's check what they'd see if they did. [Live test.] AI answers are also starting to appear in Google itself." |
| "We already have an SEO agency." | "Great, we complement them. SEO gets you ranked in links; we focus on being named in AI answers and measure it directly. Happy to work alongside them." |
| "Can you guarantee we'll be recommended?" | "No, and nobody honestly can. What I guarantee is that you'll see exactly what AI says every month, what we changed, and the trend." |
| "It's too expensive." | "What's one new customer worth to you? If the plan brings in [1–2] extra customers a month, it pays for itself. We can also start with just the audit." |
| "We'll do it ourselves." | "You can, and the audit gives you the full plan. Most clients find the ongoing tracking and content is where they need help." |
| "Let me think about it." | "Of course. What would you need to see to feel confident? I'll send the snapshot from today so you can share it internally." |

## Proposal template outline
1. Their goal (in their words)
2. What we found (3 insights from the snapshot, with screenshots)
3. Recommended package and deliverables
4. Timeline (first 30/60/90 days)
5. Investment and terms
6. Next step + signature/payment link

## Follow-up rhythm
- Same day: thank-you + snapshot/report link
- Day 3: one helpful tip
- Day 7: check-in
- Day 14: final note; set the lead status in `/app/leads`

# 06 · Marketing Plan

## Positioning statement
> For **[ICP, e.g. dental clinics in [region]]** who are losing customers to competitors that AI assistants recommend, **[Platform Name]** is an AI search optimisation service that measures what ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity say about you, fixes what keeps you out, and reports progress every month. Unlike traditional SEO agencies, we track AI answers directly with our own platform.

## Core message pillars
1. **The shift:** customers ask AI, and AI names only a few businesses.
2. **The blind spot:** you can't see what AI says about you, but we can.
3. **The method:** Measure, then Fix, then Grow, with transparent monthly reports.
4. **Honesty:** no guarantees, no hype; real data every month.

## Channels (in priority order)

### 1. LinkedIn (primary)
Detail in doc 07 (lead-generation engine). Two halves:
- **Personal brand content** (inbound): founder-led posts, 3–4 per week.
- **Targeted outreach** (outbound): personalised, value-first messages to ICP decision-makers.

**Profile setup checklist**
- [ ] Headline: "I help [ICP] get recommended by ChatGPT, Gemini & Perplexity | AI Search Optimisation"
- [ ] Banner: the outcome, plus "Free AI visibility check → [domain]"
- [ ] About section: problem → method → proof → call to action
- [ ] Featured section: free check link, a sample report (anonymised), the case study
- [ ] Company page with the same branding

**Content pillars (rotate)**
| Pillar | Example post |
|---|---|
| Live experiments | "I asked ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity for the best [service] in [city]. Here's who they named and why." |
| Teardowns | "Why AI recommends [well-known brand] in [category]: 5 things their website does right" (no attacks on small businesses) |
| How-to | "Is your website blocking ChatGPT? Check your robots.txt in 60 seconds" |
| Myths | "Ranking #1 on Google doesn't mean ChatGPT will recommend you" |
| Results | Before and after charts from client reports (with permission) |
| Behind the scenes | Building the platform; what we learn from tracking thousands of AI answers |

### 2. Free AI visibility check (lead magnet)
- Already live at `/#check`. Every post, profile and message should point to it.
- Follow up on every lead within 24 hours with a personal note about their top issue.

### 3. Content & SEO / AI-search for ourselves ("eat our own cooking")
- Our own site must score 90+ on our own audit, and our llms.txt must be published.
- Publish one article per week answering questions our buyers ask AI, for example "How do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT?", "What is GEO?" and "What is llms.txt?"
- Track our own brand as a client in the platform and show the results publicly.

### 4. Partnerships
- Web design and SEO agencies (white-label, ICP C)
- Industry associations and local business groups: offer a free talk or webinar on "AI search for [industry]"
- Complementary freelancers (copywriters, web developers): referral fee

### 5. Proof engine
- First `[3]` clients at a founding-client rate in exchange for a case study and testimonial.
- A case study template: situation → baseline visibility → what we did → visibility after 60/90 days → client quote.
- Anonymised sample report as a sales asset.

### 6. Later channels
- Webinars and workshops; podcasts in the ICP's industry; newsletters; paid LinkedIn ads with lead-gen forms once the offer converts.

## 90-day launch plan
| Weeks | Focus | Key actions |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Foundations | Choose name and niche; set prices; deploy the platform with HTTPS; set up LinkedIn profile and company page; run our own site to 90+ |
| 3–4 | Proof | Run 10 free "AI visibility snapshots" for target businesses; sign 1–3 founding clients; start posting 3×/week |
| 5–8 | Outreach | Begin a LinkedIn outreach rhythm (doc 07); 1 article per week; first webinar or talk; approach 5 agencies |
| 9–12 | Scale what works | Publish the first case study; review conversion metrics; double down on the best channel; decide on building the LinkedIn engine |

## Marketing rules
- Never claim guaranteed rankings or AI placement.
- Don't invent statistics. Use our own tracking data or cite reputable sources.
- Get written permission before publishing any client's data or name.
- Don't publicly shame small businesses in teardowns.

# Voice & copy

**We sound like:** a calm, senior advisor who tells the truth in plain English.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Clear: short sentences (< 25 words), one idea each | Jargon-heavy ("leverage LLM-driven GEO synergies") |
| Specific: numbers, names, dates | Vague ("boost your visibility!") |
| Honest: no guarantees, uncertainty stated | Hype ("dominate AI search", "#1 guaranteed") |
| Confident and quiet: no exclamation marks | Pushy: false urgency, "act now" |
| Respectful of the reader's time | Padded intros, "In today's fast-paced world…" |

## Words
- Say **"AI assistants"** (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot) to clients; "engines" only internally.
- Say **"AI search optimisation"**; mention "GEO/AEO" only once, in brackets, for marketers.
- **British spelling** (optimisation, colour), matching existing materials. Currency comes from settings.
- Headlines state the outcome: "Be the business AI recommends." Buttons state the action: "Run free check".
- Numbers: "64%", "▲ 12 pts", "13 leads", "$3,400"; dates as "November 2026".

## Honesty rules (also legal protection)
- Never promise rankings or AI placement. Promise transparency and work.
- Never invent statistics, testimonials or client logos. Mark examples as examples.
- Credit the source of any external number.

What it tracks, and booking widgets
  • Visits that arrive from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Claude, DeepSeek, Grok or Meta AI (by referrer or utm_source).
  • Form submissions that include an email, phone or message field count as leads. A lead is credited to the AI assistant the visitor came from in the last 30 days, otherwise to "other".
  • No names, emails, form contents or IP addresses are collected. The source is remembered in the visitor's browser for 30 days. In the EU/UK, mention this in the client's cookie notice.
  • Booking widgets or phone links that aren't normal forms: call window.asoLead() when a booking completes. Add data-aso-ignore to a form to skip it.
  • Google AI Overviews can't be told apart from normal Google search traffic.

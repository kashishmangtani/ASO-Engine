FAQ

Questions, answered

Is this a one-time service or ongoing?

Both. The AI Search Audit is a one-time project: you get a baseline, a technical audit and a prioritised plan, and you can implement it yourself. But AI answers aren't fixed. Models are updated, they search the web fresh for each question, and your competitors are optimising too. That's why most clients continue on a monthly plan, where we implement the fixes, publish new content, track your visibility every week and report each month.

How is this different from SEO?

Traditional SEO gets you ranked in Google's list of links. AI search optimisation (also called GEO or AEO) gets you named in the answer that an AI assistant writes. The two overlap, since good SEO helps, but AI assistants also weigh things like structured data, clear quotable answers, mentions on trusted third-party sites, and whether their crawlers are allowed on your site.

How long until I see results?

Technical fixes such as unblocking AI crawlers and adding structured data can show up within weeks for AI assistants that search the web live, like Perplexity and ChatGPT search. Building broader authority through content and citations typically takes 2–6 months. We track every week so you can see the trend.

Which AI platforms do you track?

ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini and Perplexity. The same work also helps with Google's AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot.

Can you guarantee AI will recommend me?

No one can honestly guarantee what an AI says, and you should be wary of anyone who does. What we do guarantee is transparency: you'll see exactly what AI says about you each month, what we changed, and how your numbers are moving.

Do I need to rebuild my website?

Almost never. Most improvements are additions: structured data, FAQ sections, an llms.txt file, robots.txt changes and new content. We work with WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Wix and custom sites.
